# Support

## Getting Help

### Community Support (Free)

Best for: getting started, usage questions, troubleshooting, feature discussion.

- **GitHub Discussions**: Ask questions, share ideas, show what you built ([templates provided](https://github.com/sentinel-project/sentinel/discussions))
- **GitHub Issues**: Report bugs, request features — see [docs/labels.md](docs/labels.md) for how issues are triaged
- **Documentation**: [docs/](docs/) and the [mkdocs site](https://sentinel-project.github.io/sentinel/)
- **SLA**: None — community best-effort. Well-written reports (repro steps, logs, environment) get answered fastest.

### Commercial Support

Best for: production deployments that need guaranteed response times.

- **Email**: enterprise@sentinel.dev
- **SLA**: 24-hour response for critical issues, 3 business days otherwise
- **Includes**: Priority bug fixes, deployment assistance, upgrade planning, security-patch notifications
- **Status**: Available on request while the formal enterprise edition is in planning — see [docs/enterprise.md](docs/enterprise.md)

### Custom Development & Professional Services

Best for: organizations that need capabilities beyond the roadmap.

- Custom detection content and Sigma rule onboarding
- Private plugin / integration development
- Air-gapped and regulated-environment deployment assistance
- Team training (operations + development tracks)
- **Contact**: enterprise@sentinel.dev with a short scope description; we reply with an estimate within 5 business days.

## FAQ

**Q: How do I get started?**
A: See our [Quickstart Guide](docs/quickstart.md).

**Q: How do I report a bug?**
A: Open a [GitHub Issue](https://github.com/sentinel-project/sentinel/issues) with the bug report template.

**Q: How do I contribute?**
A: See our [Contributing Guide](docs/contributing.md). Translation help: [docs/translations.md](docs/translations.md).

**Q: Is Sentinel free?**
A: Yes, Sentinel is open source under the MIT License. Commercial support is available for enterprise users.

**Q: I found a security vulnerability.**
A: **Do not open a public issue.** Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for responsible disclosure.
